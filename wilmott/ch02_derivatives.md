# ch02 derivatives notes

What is a call optin?

The right to buy a particular asset for an agreed amount at a specified time in the future

 

What is the function of the underlying asset?

Payoff function

 

What is a put option?

The right to sell an asset for an agreed amount at a specified time in the future

 

What is the payoff function of a call option?

max(S - E, 0)

 

What is the payoff function of a put option?

max(E - S, 0)
