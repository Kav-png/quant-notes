The expectation or the average of a sum is calculated:

$$E[T] = \sum_{k=0}^{n}kP(X=k)$$

$$E[T] = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}kP(X=k)$$

The formula to calculate the variance is:

$$Var(X) = E[(X-E[X])^2]$$

$$Var(X) = E[X^2] - E[X]^2$$
