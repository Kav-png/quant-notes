The expectation or the average of a sum is calculated:

$$E[T] = \sum_{k=0}^{n}kP(X=k)$$

$$E[T] = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}kP(X=k)$$

The formula to calculate the variance is:

$$Var(X) = E[(X-E[X])^2]$$

$$Var(X) = E[X^2] - E[X]^2$$

# Core Formulas

### Expected Value (Mean, *$\mu$):*

- Discrete:

$$E[X] = \sum_{}^{}cp(x)$$

- Continuous:

$$E[X] = \int_{}^{}xp(x) dx$$

### Variance ($\sigma^2$):

---

$$Var(X) = E[(X-\mu)^2]$$

### Covariance ($Cov(X, Y)$):

**Basic Probability & Rules**

- Conditional Probability: $P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$ $1$
- Bayes' Rule: $ P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A) P(B \mid A)}{P(B)} $ $2$
- Total Probability Theorem: $P(B) = \sum_{i} P(A_i) P(B \mid A_i)$ $3$, $4$
- Change of Variables for Continuous Distributions: $g(y) = \frac{p(x)}{|dy/dx|}$ $5$

**Expectations & Moments**

- Discrete Expectation: $E[f(x)] = \sum_{k} f(x_k) p(x_k)$ $6$
- Continuous Expectation: $ E[f(x)] = \int f(x) p(x) dx $ $7$
- $l$-th Moment (Continuous): $ E[x^l] = \int x^l p(x) dx $ $8$
- Variance: $ \text{Var}(x) = E[(x - \mu)^2] = E[x^2] - \mu^2 $ $9$, $10$, $11$
- Covariance: $ \text{Cov}(x,y) = E[(x-\mu_x)(y-\mu_y)] = E[xy] - E[x]E[y] $ $12$, $13$
- Correlation: $ \rho = \frac{\text{Cov}(x,y)}{\sigma_x \sigma_y} = E\left[\left(\frac{x-\mu_x}{\sigma_x}\right)\left(\frac{y-\mu_y}{\sigma_y}\right)\right] $ $14$

**Specific Probability Distributions**

- Uniform Distribution $15$ Mean and Variance: $E[x] = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \text{Var}(x) = \frac{1}{12}$ $16$
- Binomial Probability: $ P(k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k q^{n-k} $ $17$
- Binomial Mean and Standard Deviation: $ E[x] = np, \quad \sigma = \sqrt{npq} $ $18$, $19$
- Poisson Probability: $ P(k) = \frac{e^{-\lambda} \lambda^k}{k!} $ $20$
- Poisson Mean and Variance: $ E[x] = \lambda, \quad \text{Var}(x) = \lambda $ $21$, $22$
- Gaussian (Normal) Distribution PDF: $ p(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}} $ $23$, $24$
- Log-normal relationship to simple returns: $ e^r - 1 = R $ $25$

**Portfolios & Sums of Random Variables**

- Portfolio Return: $ R_p = \sum_{i=1}^n w_i R_i $ $26$
- Expected Portfolio Return: $ \mu_p = \sum_{i=1}^n w_i \mu_i $ $26$
- Portfolio Variance: $ \sigma_p^2 = \sum_{i=1}^n w_i^2 \sigma_i^2 + 2\sum_{i < j} w_i w_j \sigma_i \sigma_j \rho_{ij} $ $27$, $28$
- Equal-weighted Uncorrelated Portfolio Variance: $ \sigma_p^2 = \frac{\sigma_0^2}{n} $ $29$

**Transforms & Inequalities**

- Fourier Transform / Characteristic Function: $ \tilde{p}(t) = E[e^{itx}] = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{itx} p(x) dx $ $30$, $31$
- Binomial Characteristic Function: $ (p e^{it} + q)^n $ $32$, $33$
- Standardizing Scaling Variable (Z-score): $ z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma} $ $23$, $34$
- Markov Inequality: $ P(x \ge a) \le \frac{E[x]}{a} $ $35$
- Chebyshev Inequality: $ P(|x-\mu| \ge c) \le \frac{\sigma^2}{c^2} $ $36$
