# ch01 introduction

## Key concept

A derivative is a financial instrument hose value depends on (or derives from) the value of others
--- which are more basic underlying variables


Derivatives are trader in ***Exchange Traded Markets*** and ***Over the Counter Markets***

Exchange Traded Markets are where you have a standadized contracts that are defined by the exchange and a clearing house to ensure all contracts will be honoured

Over the Counter Markets are where a trade is agreed over a recorded call or other communication format, mostly used between financial instituitions, these are not based in the exchange and there is credit risk because there is no guarntee that both parties will honour the contract

Credit Risk is the risk that the counterparty defaults and is unable to pay the other financial instituition

OTC >>> Exchange Traded Markets

A forward contract is an OTC agreement to buy or sell a stock at a certain time in the future, there are long futures that means you buy and short futures that mean that you sell at a specific price ***K*** (called delievery price)

payoffs and derivatives 

\(Payoff_{long} = S_{T} - K\)

\(Payoff_{short} = K - S_{T}\)

the difference between payoff and overall profit is that it costs nothing to enter into a forward contract so the terminal payoff is equal to the totoal profit or loss

options have call options, put options

hedging with forwards: someones reciving payment in pounds but from usd in 3 months -> you can hedge against the FX rate by locking in 1.6 for example so if rate goes up you are covered

you can hedge with options too which acts as insurance, though requires paying premium

speculators use derivatives to bet on the futre direction of a market variable -> in essence they provide leverage so you can larger positions with a relatively small upfront capital requirement

risk profiles of futures and options are completly different as futures you can lose unlimited amount of money but with optinos you would only lose the premium you paid for the option

arbitrageurs take offsetting positions in two or more markets to lock in riskless profits

there are gaps (mainly jane street operates in this gap) where a certain stock is priced different across stock exchanges and fx rates
